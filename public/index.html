WELCOME TO VIZUALEARNER
Make mathematics tangible.
Describe any mathematical concept or attach a problem. Your local Codex CLI builds an interactive visualization in the canvas.
Keep experimenting with follow-up messages. Each exploration has its own conversation and Codex session, restored when you reopen it. Use New Visualization for a fresh start.
Everything saves automatically in your selected workspace. The Source tab lets you inspect and download generated files, and the revision menu reopens earlier versions.
Your files are stored locally. Prompts, source, and relevant attachments are sent through your configured Codex provider during generation. Generated visualizations run in an isolated frame; optional plotting libraries load from approved public CDNs.
Generated mathematics can be wrong. Check equations, assumptions and numerical results independently before relying on them.
Codex runs only when you request a generation by default. Your provider’s charges and usage limits apply. Background preparation is an optional advanced setting.
